How Do Search Engines Work?

Search Engines have two major functions, which are crawling, indexing.

Crawling

Search engines have automated robots referred to as 'crawlers' or 'spiders' which search the engine and catalog whatever they find. There are also human 'crawlers' who a search engine may rely on for ranking websites. This kind of crawling is important too because humans actually have to rank the websites based on how relevant the information is. Both kinds of crawlers are important because together they create the most efficient type of search engine.

Indexing

Indexing is the act of compiling information gathered by 'crawlers' into a database.

Searching

When you search, the engine uses the keywords you input to find relevant links in their indexed database.

What is the Importance of Keywords and Descriptions Used in Meta Tags?

Keywords and descriptions are important in meta tags because they help users find relevant websites. The more relevant keywords and descriptions used in meta tags, the more likely they will be ranked higher on search engines, resulting in more relevant internet traffic. Meta descriptions are important especially because they are the first words a user will see regarding a webpage.

Writing Relevant Content Links

Including relevant links throughout your webpage can improve search optimization. Relevant links can lead users to your sites and explore them more thoroughly and aid in better internet traffic. Higher and more relevant traffic helps websites rank higher in the search engine. Relevant content links can also help by emphasizing important information or more detailed breakdowns of concepts on separate pages within the site.

What Constitutes a Good Heading Hierarchy?

A good heading hierarchy makes a web page easy to read, and easy to navigate. The largest heading is usually saved for the title of the page, followed by smaller headings that denote sections. The smallest font usually makes up the majority of copy. It is still important however to make the smallest copy still an easily readable size.
