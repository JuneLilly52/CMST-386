About the Artist

Hi I'm Regina, and I'm the woman behind Golden Threads.

The name Golden Threads is a combination pun of my last name - Goldfarb and my love of sewing, hence Golden Threads was born. I've been sewing for about ten years now and am primarily self-taught. I've loved art my whole life and I express it through my sewing. I dabble in many varieties of sewing from historical costuming, to cosplay, vintage, and to daily wear (though for me personally the last two are essentially the same).

I love all these different styles so much I wanted to integrate them all into my own daily wardrobe. As a consumer, I found that simply wasn't a possibility. That's where Golden Threads was born. I integrated my love of all things historical, cosplay, vintage and my love of clothes in general, into a place where women can find things for their favorite fandoms, books, and movies, translated into gorgeous garments and accessories that they can wear daily without the self-conscious embarrassment of 'feeling too costumey'. The daily woman can now express herself in her love of those classic franchises, Star Wars, Lord of the Rings, Jane Austen, and much more, without compromising her wallet or ethics. My clothes are affordable, and made to last. They are designed to be worn daily, in the office, at home, out and about, these are your new staples that integrate perfectly with your existing wardrobe.

Be the woman you were born to be, and embrace your destiny wearing Golden Threads.
